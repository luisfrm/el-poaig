From Grove to Table

The Art of Excellence

01
The Pruning

We remove old branches so the tree channels all its energy into fewer fruits. The result: olives with the optimal flesh-to-pit ratio and higher antioxidant concentration.

02
The Harvest

Gentle mechanical harvesting that protects buds and bark. Branch to mill in under 4 hours, so the olive neither oxidises nor loses a single aromatic nuance.

03
The Extraction

Milled at low temperature with low extraction yield. While the industry may pursue yields around 20%, we accept only 11-14% to preserve integrity. Less quantity, but every drop retains the full integrity of its polyphenols and aromas.

Pure Preservation

"This oil is never pre-bottled. It stays protected from oxidation in stainless steel tanks without oxygen until the moment you order. Bottled on demand. Never before."
